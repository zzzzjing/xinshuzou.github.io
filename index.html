About简介

Xinshu Zou is a materials researcher in Trondheim, Norway, with more than ten years of interdisciplinary research behind him. In September 2025 he defended his PhD thesis, Anti-ice and Anti-hydrate Surfaces, at the NTNU Nanomechanical Lab in the Department of Structural Engineering, Norwegian University of Science and Technology.

His path runs across fields. During his master’s studies with the University of Science and Technology of China and the China Academy of Engineering Physics, he developed sol-gel hollow-silica coatings for high-power laser optics. At Shanghai Jiao Tong University he studied Cr-based MAX ceramics and MXenes down to the atomic scale with aberration-corrected electron microscopy. At NTNU he turned to surfaces that resist icing and gas-hydrate adhesion.

Xinshu Zou 是一名常驻挪威特隆赫姆的材料研究者，拥有十余年跨学科研究经历。2025 年 9 月，他在挪威科技大学（NTNU）结构工程系纳米力学实验室通过博士论文答辩，论文题为《Anti-ice and Anti-hydrate Surfaces》（防冰与防水合物表面）。

他的研究跨越多个领域：硕士阶段在中国科学技术大学与中国工程物理研究院联合培养项目中，研究用于高功率激光光学元件的溶胶-凝胶空心二氧化硅薄膜；在上海交通大学，借助球差校正电子显微镜在原子尺度上研究 Cr 基 MAX 相陶瓷与 MXene；在 NTNU，则转向抗结冰与抗水合物附着的功能表面。

Research研究

Three stages, one thread: how a surface’s structure decides what it does, from reflecting light to shedding ice. 三个阶段，一条主线：表面的结构如何决定其功能，从调控光的反射到让冰脱落。

  1. 2015–2018, CAEP and USTC2015–2018，中物院 / 中国科大

    Optical coatings光学薄膜

    Sol-gel coatings built from hollow silica nanoparticles: ultralow-refractive-index films, broadband antireflective stacks for high-power lasers and solar cells, transparent superhydrophobic layers, and fiber sensors for airborne contaminants. 基于空心二氧化硅纳米颗粒的溶胶-凝胶薄膜：超低折射率薄膜、用于高功率激光与太阳能电池的宽带减反射膜系、透明超疏水涂层，以及检测空气分子污染物的光纤传感器。

  2. 2018–2022, Shanghai Jiao Tong University2018–2022，上海交通大学

    MAX phases and MXenesMAX 相与 MXene

    Cr–Al–C ceramics and composites with high strength and plasticity, a simple route to Cr₂CTₓ MXene for hydrogen evolution, and nanostructured intermetallics, read at the atomic scale with aberration-corrected STEM. 兼具高强度与塑性的 Cr–Al–C 陶瓷及复合材料、用于析氢反应的 Cr₂CTₓ MXene 简易合成路线，以及纳米结构金属间化合物，并借助球差校正 STEM 开展原子尺度表征。

  3. 2022–2025, NTNU2022–2025，挪威科技大学

    Anti-ice and anti-hydrate surfaces防冰与防水合物表面

    Dynamic anti-icing surfaces made by adding β-cyclodextrin to PDMS silicone rubber, with the potential to be recharged quickly instead of replaced, alongside the mechanics of how ice debonds from a surface. 将 β-环糊精引入 PDMS 硅橡胶，构建具备快速再生潜力的可持续动态防冰表面；同时研究冰从表面脱粘的力学机制。
